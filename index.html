Questions

The things owners actually ask.

Pulled straight from our discovery calls.

Will this replace my CSRs / staff?expand_more

No. Every client we work with keeps their team. The AI handles overflow, after-hours, and the repetitive first-response work — answering instantly, collecting the right info, and booking the job — so your CSRs aren't stretched thin during busy season or stuck answering calls at 9pm. Most clients start with the AI on after-hours or overflow only, then expand once they see it booking calls reliably.

Will it sound robotic, or like it's not really from our area?expand_more

We build a custom voice for every client — tailored to your business and your region, not one generic voice reused across every company. We've matched voices to local accents (Texan, Hawaiian, etc.) and can clone a specific voice if you want it to sound like someone on your team. If the tone needs adjusting — more formal, more casual, faster, slower — we tune it until it fits.

Can it handle angry or upset customers, and emergencies?expand_more

Yes. The AI is trained to recognize frustration or urgency and adjust its response accordingly — acknowledging the issue, moving quickly to get the details it needs, and dispatching the right technician fast. If a call needs a real person — billing disputes, a situation the AI shouldn't handle — we set up rules to transfer it directly to your team.

Does it actually integrate with our CRM (ServiceTitan, ServiceFusion, Housecall Pro, etc.)?expand_more

Yes — we connect through your CRM's API, the same way for every client. The AI can recognize returning customers, create and categorize tickets, and keep your CRM as the source of truth. Integration depth depends on what each CRM's API allows, which we verify upfront before any commitment. If an API is temporarily down on your CRM's end (it happens), the AI still books the call as a new ticket and we merge it once it's back up — no missed jobs.

What if a customer is hard to understand or there's background noise?expand_more

The AI is built to filter background noise and confirm details back to the caller ("just to make sure I've got this right...") rather than asking them to repeat themselves over and over. If it genuinely can't understand after a couple of tries, it can route straight to a live person instead of frustrating the caller.

What's the pricing, and will it randomly go up?expand_more

Pricing is based on your call volume and what you want covered (after-hours only, full operations, or add-ons like SMS campaigns and outbound follow-up). We're upfront about it — no surprise increases. If your usage trends toward needing a higher tier, we give 30-45 days' notice and walk through the numbers with you before anything changes. Most clients start on a no-cap or capped-minute plan and adjust as we see real usage.

How long does setup take?expand_more

Typically about two weeks: the first week is building and testing the agent (script, voice, integrations) and getting your approval, the second week is going live and fine-tuning based on real calls. Some clients go live within a weekend for simpler after-hours setups.

Are we locked into a long-term contract?expand_more

No long-term lock-in. We typically run month-to-month after an initial setup, so if it's not working for you, you can stop with notice — no yearly contract trapping you in.

What if the AI makes a mistake or "goes rogue"?expand_more

We monitor every call and integration continuously, and we test heavily before anything goes live. If something needs a quick fix, you're talking directly to our team — not submitting a ticket into a support queue and waiting days. Calls can also be routed to a real person any time a situation calls for it.
